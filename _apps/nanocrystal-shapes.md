## Overview

Nanocrystal Shapes draws the common shapes of colloidal nanocrystals as clean, translucent 3D figures. Pick a shape, set its truncation or aspect ratio, rotate it to the view you want and export a high-resolution image — with or without a transparent background — ready for a slide, poster or paper.

It started as a single figure (a truncated tetrahedron) for my own CdSe work and grew into a general tool for explaining nanocrystal systems.

## Key features

- **13 shapes:** tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, cuboctahedron, icosahedron, decahedron (five-fold twinned), bipyramid, triangular and hexagonal plates, sphere, rod, tube and tetrapod.
- **Shape parameters:** truncation, twin planes, aspect ratio, wall thickness, end rounding and number of sides, each with its own slider.
- **Display control:** edges, faces, radii and arrows can be switched on or off; opacity, line thickness and colors are adjustable.
- **Export:** PNG, JPEG and SVG at any size, with an optional transparent background.
- **Full Help menu:** user manual, quick start, keyboard shortcuts, troubleshooting, limitations, privacy & safety and a Python-packages check.

## Who it is for

Researchers and students who need consistent, accurate nanocrystal shape drawings for figures, teaching and talks — without a 3D modelling package.
