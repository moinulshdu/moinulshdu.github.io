## Overview

Lab Icon Studio makes clean vector icons of laboratory equipment for schematics, TOC graphics and slides. Every icon is adjustable (liquid levels, colors, number of necks, sample states) and exports as SVG or PNG with a transparent background.

## Key features

- **Icons for synthesis and surface studies:** syringe, 3 / 7 / 20 mL vials with liquid, biphasic and nanocrystal states (dispersed, flocculated, pellet), NMR tube, cuvette, 1–4-neck round-bottom flasks, condensers, septum, balance, ITC cell and injection syringe, glove box, rotary evaporator, UV lamp, light rays and process arrows.
- **Nanocrystal icons:** a 2D cartoon, a 2D chemistry view with ligand head groups, and a 3D view you can rotate by dragging.
- **Presets and a Scene Composer** to combine several icons into one figure.
- **ITC animation** window that shows injection and mixing.
- **Projects and undo**, plus checkerboard, white, black or gray preview backgrounds.

## Who it is for

Chemists and materials scientists who want consistent, editable equipment drawings for papers, posters and talks.
