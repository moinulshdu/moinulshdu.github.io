## Overview

Color Inverter turns figures made for a white background into figures that work on dark slides: it inverts colors and can make the background transparent, while a split *Overlay* view shows the original and the result side by side.

## Key features

- **Invert colors** and **make the background transparent** — each can be switched on or off, and the background color can be picked.
- **Overlay view** with a draggable divider between the original and the result.
- **Drag and drop** or *File › Open*, and **batch conversion** of whole folders.
- **Nothing is saved automatically**; choose the format and location yourself.
- **Learn Colors** menu with interactive color-wheel lessons.
- Clear messages for very large images, documented size and format limits.

## Who it is for

Anyone preparing figures for dark-theme presentations, posters or websites.
