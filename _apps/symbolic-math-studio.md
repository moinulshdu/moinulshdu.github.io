## Overview

Symbolic Math Studio is a free, Maple-style worksheet for symbolic mathematics, built on SymPy. Type commands in an empty worksheet, assign equations with `eq1 := …`, and copy any result as text to paste into MATLAB, Python or a paper.

## Key features

- **Worksheet interface** like Maple: `#` comments, `restart`, `:=` assignments and typed commands such as `simplify`, `solve`, `diff` and `int`.
- **Copy results as text** to move them into other software.
- **Correctness first:** operations that SymPy cannot do reliably are deliberately left out.

## Who it is for

Researchers who derive equations (for example, binding-equilibrium models) and want a free symbolic tool after leaving a university license.
