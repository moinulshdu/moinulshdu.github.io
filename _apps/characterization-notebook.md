## Overview

Characterization Notebook is an interactive, textbook-style guide to the instruments and techniques used in nanocrystal research. Each technique covers the fundamentals, the key equations, how data are collected and how they are interpreted, with seminal papers — and you can add your own PDFs and links.

## Techniques covered

- UV–Vis absorbance spectroscopy
- Scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM)
- Powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD)
- Small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS)
- Thermogravimetric analysis (TGA)

## Key features

- Fundamentals, formulas, data collection and interpretation for each technique.
- Seminal-paper lists, plus your own PDFs and links attached to each topic.
- Same layout and Help menu as my other apps.

## Who it is for

New graduate students and researchers learning to plan, run and interpret materials characterization.
