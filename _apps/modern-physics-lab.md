## Overview

Modern Physics Lab teaches subatomic particles and atomic structure, the Schrödinger equation (particle in a box and quantum-dot nanocrystals) and special relativity (time dilation, length contraction, the Lorentz transformation and E = mc²) — step by step, written so a 12th-grade student can follow.

## Key features

- **26 lessons** revealed step by step, with **24 interactive demos** and **69 equations**.
- **Every equation has an ⓘ** listing each symbol's name, SI unit and typical value, plus where the equation comes from, with a link to the exact derivation step.
- **Quizzes after every lesson and section exams**, with randomized questions whose wrong options each represent a specific misconception; the next step unlocks at 80 %.
- Physical constants from CODATA and particle data from the Particle Data Group, with references.

## Who it is for

Chemistry and materials students who want a real understanding of the quantum and relativistic ideas behind spectroscopy and quantum dots.
