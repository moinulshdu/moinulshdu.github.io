## Overview

Audio Converter converts .m4a and other audio formats to MP3 and more — one file, a batch or a whole folder — and extracts audio from video. It also lets you cut out pauses or mistakes anywhere in a recording, much like a simple editor.

## Key features

- **Convert** between common formats; single file, batch or entire folder.
- **Extract audio** from video files.
- **Edit on the waveform:** drag to select, delete or keep only the selection, double-click to restore, undo, and zoom; cuts are sample-accurate with short fades.
- **Enhance** audio and tune bass and treble.
- **Works offline:** no internet connection is used.

## Who it is for

Anyone who records lectures, interviews or talks and needs a quick, private way to clean up and convert audio.
