## Overview

Instrument Lab is an interactive learning app for the electronics behind device measurements: the oscilloscope, function generator, lock-in amplifier and current–voltage (I–V) characterization of solar cells, photodetectors and diodes — the instrumentation used to make and test nanocrystal devices.

## Key features

- **Live demos** of each instrument with realistic controls, each with a *Reset to default* button.
- **I–V curves** for solar panels, photodetectors, diodes and more.
- **Textbook explanations** open from an ⓘ icon, so text never covers the controls.
- Same Help menu as my other apps: user guide, packages, privacy & safety and About.

## Who it is for

Students and researchers who want to become confident with lab electronics before using the real instruments.
